Petly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

1. Overview

Petly ("the App") helps you track pet vaccinations, treatments, feeding, and reminders. The App is designed to keep your pet records on your device. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not:

  • collect, transmit, or upload pet records to our server
  • collect human medical or health data
  • use analytics SDKs, other than the data processed by advertising services described in Section 4
  • track your in-app behavior for our own analytics
  • sell your data

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Information you enter — pet profiles, vaccinations, treatments, feeding plans, reminders, notes, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, in the App's private database. This data is not uploaded to us and is not shared with third parties by the App.

4. Advertising

The free version of the App shows a banner ad and may offer optional, opt-in rewarded video ads (shown only when you explicitly choose to watch one). Ads can be removed with an optional in-app purchase.

Ads are served through the Appodeal ad mediation platform, which delivers ads from multiple third-party ad networks and demand partners participating in its mediation and ad exchange. The specific networks may vary by region and change between App updates; the list of Appodeal's advertising partners is available via Appodeal's privacy documentation.

To show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, these services may process limited device and advertising data, such as your advertising identifier (e.g. Google Advertising ID), IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it, device and network information, ad interaction events, and diagnostic data. This processing is governed by the providers' own privacy policies:

  • Appodeal Privacy Policy (including its list of advertising partners)

We do not send your pet records, reminders, notes, or feeding data to advertising services.

Where required by law (e.g. GDPR in the EEA/UK), you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can decline personalized advertising. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier and limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Purchases

If you buy the optional ad removal purchase, the purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment card details.

6. Notifications

The App may send local notifications for pet care reminders. These notifications are generated on your device and do not require sending your pet records to any server.

7. Backup

If the App offers backup or export features, any backup file is created on your device and stored only where you choose to save or share it. We have no access to your backup files.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
