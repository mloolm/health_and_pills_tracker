Privacy Policy

Application: Classic Solitaire Set (com.solitaireplatform.solitairepack)
Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy explains what data the Classic Solitaire Set mobile application (the “App”) collects, why, and what choices you have. Please read it before using the App.

In short. The App has no accounts, no registration and no login. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data leaving your device is collected by our advertising partner, Appodeal, and its ad networks, so that ads can be shown and measured.

1. Who we are

The App is published by Solitaire Platform (the “we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, write to krasheninin.p.a@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device only

The App saves the following locally, using the operating system’s standard app storage. It is not transmitted to us and we have no access to it:

  • game progress, statistics, win streaks and level;
  • in-game coin balance and rewards;
  • your settings, such as language, card design and sound.

This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the system settings.

3. We do not operate accounts or servers

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact detail. We do not run a game server, we do not sync your progress to the cloud, and we do not build profiles about you ourselves.

4. Advertising

The App is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are delivered through the Appodeal SDK (Appodeal Inc.), which mediates demand from a number of third-party advertising networks. The App shows banner ads, interstitial ads and opt-in rewarded video ads.

To serve, cap, measure and bill for ads, Appodeal and the ad networks it works with may collect and process:

CategoryExamples
Device identifiers Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID, App Set ID), and identifiers generated by the ad SDK
Device and technical data Device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen parameters, language and region settings, network type, carrier, IP address
Coarse location Approximate location derived from the IP address. The App does not request GPS or precise location permission
Ad interaction data Which ads were requested, shown, viewed, clicked or dismissed; session and frequency data used for capping and fraud prevention

This processing is carried out by Appodeal and its partners as independent controllers or processors under their own privacy policies. Please review:

  • Appodeal Privacy Policy — https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy/
  • Appodeal’s current list of demand partners and their privacy policies — https://www.appodeal.com/home/privacy-policy/demand-partners/

The set of ad networks in the mediation stack can change over time; the list linked above is the authoritative and current one.

5. Consent and your advertising choices

Where the law requires it — in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the Appodeal SDK presents a consent request when the App first starts, and processes personalised advertising only on the basis of the consent you give. You may decline; the App remains fully playable, and you will see non-personalised or contextual ads instead.

Independently of that, you can at any time:

  • reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android;
  • clear the App’s data in system settings, which also clears any stored consent choice, so the request will be shown again the next time you launch it;
  • write to us at the address in section 1 and we will help you exercise your rights.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and for any related profiling;
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for showing non-personalised ads, for capping how often an ad is shown, for measurement and for preventing fraud and abuse.

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

7. California and other US state privacy rights

We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing device identifiers with advertising partners for cross-context behavioural advertising may nevertheless be treated as a “sale” or “sharing” under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws. California residents have the right to know, to delete, to correct, and to opt out of such sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To opt out, decline personalised ads in the consent request, enable the system-level ad personalisation opt-out on your device, or contact us at the address in section 1.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

9. International transfers

Our advertising partners operate globally, so the data described in section 4 may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Those transfers are carried out by the partners under the safeguards set out in their own privacy policies, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention

Data stored on your device stays until you delete the App or clear its data. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies, linked in section 4.

11. Security

The App requests no dangerous runtime permissions and stores no credentials. Data in transit between the ad SDK and its servers is protected by transport encryption provided by those services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App’s functionality or our partners change. The current version is always available at this address, and the date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected in that date; please check back from time to time.
